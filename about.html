About Store

A shop built on listening, not just selling.

Shree Sai Musical started as a one-room tabla repair counter in 1998. Today it's a full instrument house — but the counter is still the same one.

Our Story

From a repair table to a full house of instruments

My father, Ramesh Joshi, opened Shree Sai Musical in 1998 to repair tabla and harmonium for local artists who had nowhere else to go. Word spread through the city's music circles, and within a decade the small counter had grown into a proper shop — one that sold instruments as carefully as it repaired them.

We still test-play every instrument that comes through our door, and we still believe the right instrument is the one that fits the player — not the one with the highest price tag.

Milestones

27 years, one address

1998 — The repair counter opens

A single table, a soldering iron, and a reputation for fixing tabla nobody else could.

2006 — First retail floor

Shree Sai Musical moves into its current Station Road address and begins selling instruments alongside repairs.

2014 — Lessons begin

In-house teachers start weekend classes for harmonium, tabla, and guitar.

2026 — Second generation

Now run with the founder's daughter at the counter, still tuning every instrument by ear.
