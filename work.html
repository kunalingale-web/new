How A Repair Works

From drop-off to delivery

1. Diagnosis

Bring the instrument in — we assess it on the spot and quote a fair repair cost, no surprises later.

2. Workbench

Repairs are done in-house by our own craftsmen, most within 3–7 days depending on the work.

3. Tuning & test-play

Every repaired instrument is tuned and played before it's handed back — we don't return anything we haven't tested.

4. Pickup

You get a call the day it's ready, plus care notes so the fix lasts.
